# Client Brief — Glow Cosmetics

## Client
Mrs. Uyi Edokpolo — Glow Cosmetics, New Benin, Benin City, Nigeria.

## Problem
The shop receives approximately 50 customer questions each week asking what cream to use for their skin. Most concern dark spots and dry skin; some ask for bleaching creams, which Glow Cosmetics does not sell.

## Never Rule
Never give medical advice or recommend products for skin problems. Trained staff member Grace replies to all skin questions, reactions, or allergy mentions. Direct customers to **Enquire / Book** so Grace can respond.

## Top 5 FAQs
1. **Are your products original?** Yes, 100% authentic.
2. **What cream should I use for my skin/dark spots/dry skin?** Please enquire or book so Grace can help. The website does not recommend products for skin concerns.
3. **Do you sell bleaching creams?** No, Glow Cosmetics does not sell bleaching creams.
4. **Do you deliver to [City]?** Yes. Nationwide delivery is available across Nigeria, with Benin City pickup and delivery.
5. **How do I order?** Select items or click Enquire / Book, confirm availability and delivery details with the team, and complete payment.
