Frequently asked questions

Need more help? Send the team an enquiry and we’ll point you in the right direction.

Talk to our team →
Are your products original?

Yes. Glow Cosmetics offers 100% authentic products.

What cream should I use for my skin, dark spots or dry skin?

We don’t recommend products for skin problems online. Please Enquire / Book so Grace, our trained staff member, can respond to your question.

Do you sell bleaching creams?

No. We do not sell bleaching creams.

Do you deliver to my city?

Yes. We offer nationwide delivery across Nigeria, plus pickup and delivery in Benin City.

How do I place an order?

Choose products and quantities with Order, enter your delivery details, and place the order through WhatsApp. The team confirms availability and payment details with you.
