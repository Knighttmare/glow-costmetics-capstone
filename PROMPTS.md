# Glow Cosmetics — Creative Prompts

## Consistent Editorial Visual Style Anchor
Use a refined, warm, editorial skincare-shop aesthetic: natural daylight, soft shadows, tactile ivory and warm-sand surfaces, deep botanical green accents, restrained warm-gold details, clean composition, realistic product photography, generous negative space, calm and welcoming. Keep imagery authentic and inclusive for a Nigerian beauty retailer. Do not show medical claims, before/after transformations, skin lightening, bleaching products, or text/logos baked into the image. Maintain the same color grade and quiet premium feel across every image.

## UI Prompts

- Build a mobile-first, accessible static storefront in semantic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. Use warm ivory `#FAF7F2`, warm sand `#F3ECE2`, botanical green `#1F332A`, and warm gold `#B8863B`; prioritize readable contrast, generous spacing, responsive layouts, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support.
- Keep the navigation sticky and compact on small screens; make all actions reachable by touch and use a clear enquiry path for personalised questions.
- Use image placeholders that remain polished when image assets are absent. Avoid invented product claims, medical advice, skin-problem recommendations, and bleaching products.
- Make FAQ rows keyboard-operable with accessible expanded state. Keep copy concise, friendly, and consistent with the approved client brief.
